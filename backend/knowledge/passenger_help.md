## 1. Navigating BusSense AI
- **Live Departures (Home Tab):** View live bus countdowns, cabin crowding badges, active corridor advisories, and nearby departures.
- **Explore Corridors (Explore Tab):** Search origin and destination stops, review corridor route alternatives, and explore transit recommendations.
- **Digital Tickets (Tickets Tab):** View active digital boarding passes with QR codes, review past trip history, and track active journey HUDs.
- **AI Assistant (AI Tab):** Real-time conversational transit assistant grounded in verified network knowledge, live bus telemetry, ticket records, and crowd forecasting.

## 2. Active Journey Heads-Up Display (HUD)
- **Commuter-First Hierarchy:**
  1. Current Bus & Route Number
  2. Passenger's Final Destination Stop
  3. Next Approaching Stop
  4. Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA in minutes)
  5. Route Progress milestone line
- **Quick QR Access:** Tap "Show Digital QR" inside the HUD anytime conductor inspection is requested.

## 3. Passenger Troubleshooting & Offline Mode
- **QR Code Won't Scan:** Ensure screen brightness is high. If optical scanning fails, conductors can manually verify the 6-character ticket reference code.
- **Offline Mode:** Previously booked digital passes stored in local device cache can be viewed offline. Real-time GPS tracking and AI queries require an active data connection.
- **Missing Bus Information:** If a bus is delayed or telemetry is interrupted, the app displays `ESTIMATE` or `LIMITED DATA` rather than inventing false locations.

## 4. Research Prototype Disclosures & State Policy Boundaries
- **Prototype Status:** BusSense AI is an intelligent transit research prototype for Greater Kochi corridors.
- **Official Transport Policy Disclosures:**
  - Official KSRTC administrative concessions, staff duty rosters, and government helpline numbers are outside the app's verified scope.
  - Inter-district KSRTC Swift, Minnal, or Super Fast services operating outside Greater Kochi are not tracked.
  - Cash refunds and official grievance redressals must be directed to respective depot station masters.
  - When asked about official policies not covered in this guide, the assistant will state that it does not have enough reliable information.
